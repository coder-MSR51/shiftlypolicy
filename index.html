Privacy Policy for Shiftly

Effective Date: November 18, 2026

Manish Singh ("we," "our," or "us") built the Shiftly app as a free utility application. This page is used to inform users regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information if anyone decides to use our Service.

1. Information Collection and Use

Shiftly is designed with user privacy as a top priority. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or user files.

  • All image compression, PDF conversion, and file editing operations are performed 100% locally on your device.
  • Your photos, documents, and PDFs are never uploaded to any external server or cloud storage.

2. File Access and Permissions

To provide core features such as selecting images or PDFs for compression or conversion, the app uses Android's standard system file picker. The app only accesses the specific files that you explicitly select and displays only the files processed within the app. We do not scan or access your device's broader storage without your action.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertisements

Currently, Shiftly does not contain any advertisements and does not integrate any third-party analytics, tracking tools, or data-collecting SDKs. (Note: If third-party services such as Google AdMob are added in future updates, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly).

4. Data Security

Since all file processing happens offline directly on your phone, your files remain completely under your control on your device's local storage.

5. Children’s Privacy

Our Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Any changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.
